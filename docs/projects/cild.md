# CILD — Cost-Informed Latent Dynamics

CILD is an ongoing research project built on top of TD-MPC2.

## Motivation

Many latent world models are optimized for prediction, but prediction alone may
not be enough for planning.

CILD explores whether latent dynamics models can become more useful for control
when they are trained with planner-aware cost information.

## Current Focus

- Latent dynamics modeling
- Cost prediction in latent space
- Planner-aware representation learning
- TD-MPC2-style control

## Status

Ongoing.
